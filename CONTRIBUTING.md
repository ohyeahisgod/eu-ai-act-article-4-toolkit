# Contributing

Corrections and practical improvements are welcome.

## Source standard

- Use primary sources for legal and regulatory claims.
- Include a direct link and the date the source was checked.
- Distinguish binding text from guidance, examples and operational recommendations.
- Do not claim that a template, certificate or course guarantees compliance.
- Do not submit personal data, client records, confidential examples or proprietary training material.

## Change standard

Keep templates vendor-neutral and usable without Prooflane. Explain what changed, why it helps an operator and how it was checked. By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under the repository's MIT License.
