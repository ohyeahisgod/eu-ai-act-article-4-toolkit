# EU AI Act Article 4 operational toolkit

Free, editable templates for turning an AI literacy programme into a maintained operational record.

This toolkit is for organisations that provide or deploy AI systems and for advisors helping them organise Article 4 work. It focuses on the practical connections between AI systems, people, learning measures, policies, completion records and reviews.

## Start here

1. List every AI system in [`templates/ai-system-inventory.csv`](templates/ai-system-inventory.csv).
2. Map the people who build, operate, review or govern those systems with [`templates/ai-literacy-training-matrix.csv`](templates/ai-literacy-training-matrix.csv).
3. Adapt [`templates/responsible-ai-use-policy.md`](templates/responsible-ai-use-policy.md) to your approved tools and actual risks.
4. Record dated measures and follow-up in [`templates/article-4-evidence-register.csv`](templates/article-4-evidence-register.csv).
5. Use the [`30-day implementation guide`](guides/30-day-implementation-guide.md) to test whether a reviewer can follow the evidence without relying on interviews.
6. See how the records connect in the [complete fictional recruitment AI evidence pack](examples/fictional-recruitment-ai-evidence-pack.md).

## What the official guidance says

The European Commission's current AI literacy Q&A says organisations should consider:

- whether they are providers or deployers;
- the risks and context of the AI systems;
- people's technical knowledge, experience, education and training;
- the people who operate or use systems on the organisation's behalf; and
- the people or groups affected by the systems.

The Commission also says there is no single mandatory training format. The current framework retains an obligation for providers and deployers to take AI literacy measures but does not prescribe one specific “sufficient” level.

Primary sources:

- [European Commission — AI literacy questions and answers](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/ai-literacy-questions-answers)
- [EU AI Act Service Desk — Article 4](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-4)
- [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)

Sources last checked: 17 August 2026.

## What these files do not prove

Completing a template does not establish that Article 4 applies, determine an organisation's legal role, classify an AI system, prove that measures are appropriate, or guarantee compliance. The files are operational starting points and not legal advice. Confirm material legal and high-risk-system decisions with qualified counsel and accountable owners.

## Why the records connect

A completion list alone cannot explain why a measure fit the work. A useful evidence trail should connect:

`AI system → purpose and risk → person or role → learning measure → assessment or acknowledgement → policy version → review trigger`

[Prooflane](https://prooflane.coincroupier.workers.dev/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repository&utm_campaign=article4-toolkit&utm_content=readme) is the subscription workspace for maintaining those connections over time. The templates remain free to use independently.
